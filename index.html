Loan Application Procedures

Fill in the Application Form

Submit the application form or visit our office with relevant documents. We review your application within one day. For a faster process, respond to all questions and check the boxes provided in the form.

📝

Screening Process

The responsible officer reviews your documents and responds accordingly. Once satisfied, your application moves to the next stage. If anything is missing, we will notify you immediately with guidance.

🔍

Wait for the Outcome

After passing the screening stage, you'll be informed and asked to submit the required documents. The entire process takes a maximum of three days when documents are provided on time.

✅

Receive Your Loan

After approval, disbursement is made through your bank account or by issuing an open cheque. Depending on your bank, funds may take some hours to appear in your account.
