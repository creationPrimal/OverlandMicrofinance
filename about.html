About Us

How We Started

The establishment of Overland Microfinance Limited stems from the long-standing partnership between its co-founders, Mr. Ejaga Joseph Mahamba and Mr. Stephen Shinday Jilala. Their journey began during their Advanced Level studies in the PCB (Physics, Chemistry, and Biology) combination, where they developed strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
They later pursued different but complementary academic paths—Mr. Mahamba earned a Bachelor of Accounting and Finance from Mzumbe University, while Mr. Jilala obtained a Bachelor of Science with Education from the University of Dar es Salaam. Professionally, Mr. Mahamba worked as an auditor at the Tanzania Revenue Authority, gaining expertise in finance and compliance, while Mr. Jilala served as a government education officer, building experience in leadership and community engagement.
Their paths later converged in Dar es Salaam, where they ventured into agribusiness, managing livestock such as cows, sheep, and goats. This experience strengthened their skills in financial planning, risk management, and investment. Motivated by the financial challenges faced by local entrepreneurs, they transitioned into the financial services sector and established Overland Microfinance Limited to provide accessible credit solutions and support economic growth within their community.

Introduction

Overland Microfinance Limited is a financial institution established in 2026 and based in Goba Njia Nne, Ubungo Municipality. Founded by Mr. Ejaga Joseph Mahamba and Mr. Stephen Shinday Jilala, the company draws on over 15 years of experience in tax administration and commercial banking to address credit gaps in Tanzania’s informal and formal sectors.

It offers a range of products, including micro-loans and asset financing for boda boda, bajaji, and agricultural equipment. Beyond lending, Overland also provides business support services such as incubation, tax advisory, and professional development to promote long-term client success.
